1. Data Collection & Privacy

This website is a static developer portfolio and project showcase. It does not collect, store, or process personal user data through tracking cookies, user accounts, or background profiling scripts.

When using the contact form, your input is converted into a direct email draft handled locally on your device via your default mail client. No message details or personal contact information are stored on server databases by this website.

2. Browser Caching & Performance

To deliver fast load times and smooth page transitions, your web browser may temporarily cache static assets locally on your device, including:

  • JavaScript modules (`main.js`, `products.js`, `stores.js`)
  • CSS stylesheets (`style.css`, `tokens.css`)
  • Web fonts, SVG icons, and webp graphics

This is standard web behavior managed directly by your browser. You can clear cached files at any time through your browser settings.

3. External Links & Third-Party Services

This portfolio contains links to external platforms and services, including:

  • GitHub and GitHub Pages live deployments (`jcsorillo.github.io`)
  • Direct messaging channels (WhatsApp, Viber, Email)
  • Professional networking profiles (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
  • Google Fonts for typography styling

When navigating to third-party platforms via these external links, your interactions are governed by the privacy policies and terms of those respective platforms. I do not have control over data handled by third-party services.

This privacy policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements to website functionality or hosting requirements.
